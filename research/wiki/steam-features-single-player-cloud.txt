 Mortal Shell 2 Steam Features: Cloud, Controller and Single-Player Support
 What the official Mortal Shell 2 Steam listing confirms about single-player support, Steam Cloud, controllers, Family Sharing and unannounced online features.
Last verified 2026-09-01 
 The live Steam listing for Mortal Shell II has a compact but useful feature block. It currently lists Single-player , Steam Achievements , Full Controller Support , DualSense Controller Support , Steam Cloud , and Family Sharing . These are storefront features, not a substitute for a full launch-day settings guide, so this page separates what they confirm from what they do not.
 Official Steam artwork. Steam's feature categories confirm the PC storefront support listed below; they do not describe every in-game option. 
 Confirmed on the Steam listing
 Steam category What it safely tells you 
 Single-player 
 Steam currently markets the PC game as a single-player experience.
 Steam Achievements 
 The Steam version has achievements; the public listing does not disclose their names or conditions.
 Full Controller Support 
 A controller can be used throughout the Steam version.
 DualSense Controller Support 
 DualSense support is listed. Steam does not specify which adaptive-trigger, haptic, or wired/wireless behaviours are implemented.
 Steam Cloud 
 Steam Cloud support is listed for the PC version. The listing does not explain save-slot count, conflict handling, or backup limits.
 Family Sharing 
 The Steam listing marks the game as compatible with Family Sharing.
 What this does not confirm
 The same official listing does not announce co-op, PvP, multiplayer matchmaking, cross-play, or cross-save between PC and consoles. A Steam Cloud label is not cross-save: it describes the Steam version’s cloud feature and says nothing about transfers to PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S.
 It also does not publish a complete control-remapping, accessibility, vibration, or graphics-options matrix. Do not treat an icon in the Steam feature block as proof of a specific setting until Cold Symmetry or Playstack documents it.
 A practical retail-build check
 Use the official Steam page to confirm the feature tags in your own region before purchase.
 If you plan to use a controller, check the final build’s in-game prompt style and remapping options rather than assuming all DualSense features are present.
 Enable Steam Cloud only after checking your local save path and available storage; a cloud feature reduces risk but is not a replacement for a backup policy.
 Treat any claim about co-op, PvP, cross-play, or console-to-PC progression as unconfirmed until an official feature list names it.
