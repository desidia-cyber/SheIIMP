 Mortal Shell 2 Troubador's Lute
 Troubador's Lute in Mortal Shell 2: One-Legged Wolf Tavern location, how Confusion makes enemies fight each other, and its +100% Balance Patch 1 buff.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Troubador’s Lute is the strangest sidearm in Mortal Shell 2: instead of raw damage, it applies Confusion , making enemies attack each other for a short time. Balance Patch 1 doubled its attack damage (+100%), the biggest single buff in that patch.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Sidearm – Confusion instrument
 Region
 One-Legged Wolf Tavern, eastern Fainweald
 Unlock achievement
 None
 Resource
 Resolve
 Where to find it
 From the Mushroom Village gate, take the bridge north.
 Cleanse the corruption blocking the path – you need siphoned Ova (Mether’s Breath).
 Follow the path to the tavern, south-east of the One-Legged Wolf beacon, and take the lute to the left of the musician on the stage.
 Gragu is in the same building, so you can start his quest on the same visit.
 How to use it
 Best in corridors and packed camps: play it, step back, and let the room thin itself out.
 Resetting a bad pull costs only Resolve.
 It does little against a lone boss.
