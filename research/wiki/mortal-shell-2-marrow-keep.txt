 Mortal Shell 2 Marrow Keep
 Marrow Keep in Mortal Shell 2: Zhirelle, Franz and the Tarforge, Merrick's Rare Findings, the Gloom Siphon, Milos, Thestus and a first-visit checklist.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 Marrow Keep is Mortal Shell 2’s hub – the fortress you return to between every Corrupted Gate. Almost every system in the game runs through one of its NPCs.
 Who is in Marrow Keep
 NPC / station What it does 
 Zhirelle , the Shellkeeper
 Shell upgrades with Glimpse, marking Shells on the map (3 Glimpse), Shell Severance
 Franz and the Tarforge 
 Weapon upgrades – give him the Muradean Actuator first; sidearms also need the Obsidian Lathe
 Merrick 
 Shop: consumables, location maps, and the Rare Findings tab (Mether’s Severance, 5,000 coins)
 Gloom Siphon 
 Siphon Ova; five more earns Mether’s Breath. After the final boss, “Send Ova” starts New Game Plus (no confirmation prompt)
 Milos 
 Opens The Unfound Path once you have about 160 Ova
 Thestus 
 Calls the night (Night Mode) after you light the Gloombound Flame on top of the keep
 Genessa 
 Her Shell, trainer and bond Memories after you deliver Sester’s Censer
 Ova Nursery 
 Leads to the lift, the two portals and later The Unfound Path
 First visit checklist
 Offer the Ovum at the gate to get in.
 Give Franz the Muradean Actuator from the Village Outskirts beacon chest.
 Talk to Merrick – the Forgotten Crossbow map is free on your first conversation.
 Take the north portal . The corrupted south portal is a much harder area.
 Endgame uses
 The Tarforge cap is +16 until you bring back the Endless Core from the Hidden Nave.
 Trade items from the Unfound Path guardians (Offspring Trophy, Monolith Fragment, Orrem’s Head) sell for 5,000 coins each.
 In NG+ the keep is your restart point – the prologue is skipped.
 Patch notes
 The 6 September update made Zhirelle comment on the corruption in Marrow Keep, stopped Merrick’s greeting repeating and the Tarforge talking over itself. The 14 September hotfix fixed multiple Muttons spawning in the hub and Zhirelle not interacting in NG+; 24 September optimised shadows in the hub.
