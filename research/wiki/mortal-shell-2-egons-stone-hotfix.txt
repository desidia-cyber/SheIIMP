Mortal Shell 2 Egon's Stone Hotfix: Dungeons, Gloom, and Seeking the Past 
Skip to content
 Egon's Stone was breaking more than one dungeon
 Mortal Shell 2 Egon's Stone bugs and fixes: dungeons not loading, open-world Gloom deleted, the Seeking the Past fix and the October 13 Gloom-loss exploit.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 Egon’s Stone is a Tarstone that interacts with Gloom – and for most of September it was the most bug-reported item in Mortal Shell 2. Three hotfixes touched it. If a dungeon would not load or your Gloom vanished, this page tells you which fix you need.
 The fixes in order
 Hotfix What it fixed 
 14 September
 Egon’s Stone Gloom exploit closed; dungeons not loading correctly after using Egon’s Stone
 24 September
 Dungeons not loading when using Egon’s Stone; Egon’s Stone removing Gloom dropped in the open world; Seeking the Past trophy/achievement issues
 13 October
 Unequipping Egon’s Stone could prevent Gloom loss (the reverse exploit)
 Symptoms and what to do
 A dungeon door loads forever or drops you back outside. Make sure you are on the 24 September hotfix or later, then re-enter with the stone unequipped once to confirm the dungeon loads.
 Gloom you dropped on the ground is gone. Before 24 September the stone could delete open-world Gloom piles. That Gloom is not coming back; re-farm it. The Gloom farming guide has the best spots (the pod cliff on The Unfound Path is excellent).
 You never lose Gloom on death. That was the 13 October exploit; it is closed now.
 Seeking the Past will not unlock. Patch first, then talk to Genessa directly for her Memories – see the Seeking the Past page .
 Related stones
 The 14 September hotfix also fixed Justiciar Stone durability, Gloombound Stone losing durability from non-enemy Gloom, and made the Colossus Stone cancellable mid-execution. The 6 September update made Fragile Tarstones grant Tarcores when they break.
