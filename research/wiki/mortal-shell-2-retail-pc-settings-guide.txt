 Mortal Shell 2 PC Settings: Requirements, Storage and Stutter Checks
 Mortal Shell II PC settings guide covering Windows, 16 GB RAM, the listed GPU tier, 70 GB SSD and safe post-launch troubleshooting steps.
Last verified 2026-09-08 
 The released Mortal Shell II PC listing asks for Windows 10 or 11, 16 GB of RAM, a graphics card around RTX 2060 SUPER or RX 6600 class, and 70 GB of available storage. Those are the published requirement tiers, not a guarantee of one frame rate on every system.
 Start with a stable baseline and change one setting at a time so a stutter report is reproducible. 
 Safe first checks
 Install the game and the latest platform patch on an SSD with more than the listed 70 GB free.
 Update the graphics driver for the installed GPU.
 Disable overlays one at a time if frame pacing changes.
 Test a repeatable path rather than judging the menu or a single cinematic.
 Record resolution, upscaling, frame cap, GPU, RAM and patch version before reporting a fix.
 Avoid copying an .ini tweak without knowing what it changes. A setting that improves one GPU can create texture, input or stability problems on another.
 Why launch guides can be stale
 Mortal Shell II received a Balance Patch 1 and a Week 1 Update after launch. They are gameplay-focused, but any post-launch build can also change loading, shader compilation or frame pacing. Always compare the installed build and the publication date of a troubleshooting page.
 The PC requirements page lists the hardware tiers. Use the blurry graphics fix and reflex stutter fix pages only after capturing your current settings.
