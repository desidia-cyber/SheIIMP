 Mortal Shell 2 Triarch Repeater
 Triarch Repeater in Mortal Shell 2: Blackwell Cavern location from Castigator's Keep, burst fire, the Resolve change, and why it is a Hexapod favourite.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Triarch Repeater fires long automatic bursts. Individual bullets are weak, but sustained fire shreds weak enemies and keeps big targets like Hexapod under pressure.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Sidearm – automatic repeater
 Region
 Blackwell Cavern, near Castigator’s Keep (Ruins of Mammon)
 Unlock achievement
 Old Painless (39.0% of players)
 Resource
 Resolve
 Where to find it
 Drop down behind the Castigator’s Keep Beacon to the lower level.
 Follow the cliffside path and take the wooden lift down.
 Circle the grassy field until the cave mouth appears.
 The cavern is very dark – lock on to each wraith to see it coming.
 Take the repeater from beside a corpse in the first lit chamber.
 How to use it
 Pair it with a damage-over-time Tarstone so every bullet stacks status.
 The Proxima support summon uses it against Hexapod; you can do the same – shoot the face when the appendages part.
 Patch history
 Balance Patch 1: no longer has a minimum Resolve requirement to fire.
