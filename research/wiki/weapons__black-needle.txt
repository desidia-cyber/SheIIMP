 Mortal Shell 2 Black Needle
 Black Needle in Mortal Shell 2: Sester's Bastion bells, the Twin and Stray Sesters fight, the spear's throw, and the Point Taken achievement.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Black Needle is a spear and one of only three boss-drop primary weapons. Its achievement reads “Unlock Black Needle”, which made many players think it was a Shell – it is not.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Primary weapon – spear with throw
 Region
 Sester’s Bastion, via Sester’s Gate (Ruins of Mammon)
 Unlock achievement
 Point Taken (38.1% of players)
 Drop from
 Twin Sesters → Stray Sester
 Ability Tarstone
 Conqueror’s Reward (from Droeg)
 Where to find it
 Enter the dungeon from the Sester’s Gate Beacon (chest of coins at the entrance) and take the elevator down.
 Broken paths rebuild when you hit bells with your sidearm. The bridge is broken in three places – ring all three bells.
 In the large arena defeat the Twin Sesters , then the Stray Sester .
 The Black Needle drops with Sester’s Crest .
 How to use it
 Long thrusts outrange most humanoid enemies – fight Sesters and Confessors at the tip.
 Light attack tracking improved in Balance Patch 1.
 It has a throw; the 13 October hotfix fixed the throw and infused stone being unable to aim or interact.
