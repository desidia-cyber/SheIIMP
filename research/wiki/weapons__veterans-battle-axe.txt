 Mortal Shell 2 Veteran's Battle Axe
 Veteran's Battle Axe in Mortal Shell 2: King's Crypt location from the Shattered Beacon, the spin attack for Resolve, and the Cut You Down to Size achievement.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Veteran’s Battle Axe is a heavy two-handed axe found at the end of the King’s Crypt . Its spin attack is a favourite for refilling Resolve, which is why it shows up in so many Genessa and boss-cheese builds.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Primary weapon – heavy battle axe
 Region
 The King’s Crypt, Blackridge Pass area (Fainweald)
 Unlock achievement
 Cut You Down to Size (59.3% of players)
 Ability Tarstone
 Lost Clotstone (from The Lost Child)
 Where to find it
 Travel to Blackridge Pass and head to the Shattered Beacon (Proxima’s Shell is also here).
 Go north-west of the Shattered Beacon to the crypt entrance.
 Work through the traps and enemies to the large square opening near the end.
 Drop down, defeat the enemy below and take the axe from the dead soldier (some guides describe it as a sarcophagus at the crypt’s end).
 How to use it
 The spin attack hits everything around you and returns a lot of Resolve – the core of the Genessa method against Malborn Offspring.
 Heavy weapons received a poise and damage pass in the Week 1 Update.
 Slow recoveries: Harden before you commit a full combo.
