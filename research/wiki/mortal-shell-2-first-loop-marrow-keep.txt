Mortal Shell 2: The First Loop at Marrow Keep 
Skip to content
 Wake up, siphon, then leave the keep
 After the Tar Golem, Mortal Shell 2 starts at Marrow Keep. Siphon Ova, learn the swap, then take one gate.
Last verified 2026-10-16 
 You wake as the Harbinger. Harros is gone. Marrow Keep is the hub: the Gloom Siphon, Zhirelle for Shell swaps, Merrick for maps and Mether’s Severance , and the Tarforge.
 Siphon Ova here. About five more earns Mether’s Breath , which is fast travel between cleansed beacons and the tool that clears corrupted growth. Do not walk the whole map before that unlock if a beacon is already cleansed behind you. Map and fast travel .
 One loop, then a gate
 Talk to the keep NPCs before you pick a dungeon. Merrick’s maps mark weapon pins if you would rather spend Coins than search.
 Swap into the first Shell you actually found. Do not offer at Smert’s altar as Harbinger. First swap .
 Pick one gate. The six gates are not a forced order once Mether’s Breath is up. One clear teaches the seal and the weapon better than three half-runs.
 Gloom drops on death and can be picked back up. Do not treat a first death as a wiped file.
