What to Do After You Die in Mortal Shell 2 
Skip to content
 Pick up the Gloom, then use the beacon
 Death in Mortal Shell 2 drops Gloom. Recover it, Harden the next tell, and do not restart the file.
Last verified 2026-10-18 
 Death is not a wiped file. Gloom drops and can be recovered. A Glimpse spent to summon a Shell spirit in a boss room is the cost of that summon, not a punishment for dying.
 Do not start a new game because the first gate killed you. Go back to the last cleansed beacon.
 After the death screen
 Retrieve the Gloom before you pull a second elite. A second death can cost the pile.
