 This Mortal Shell 2 dungeons and open world guide is for players deciding whether to follow the main route or investigate side paths. Steam describes the world as compact, tightly interconnected, and designed to reward wandering with new Shells, weaponry, upgrades, and curiosities.

 Should you skip optional dungeons?
 Not automatically. The official store description places useful progression rewards away from the golden path, so an optional dungeon may be relevant even when it is not required for the next story objective. If you are stuck, explore one nearby branch before assuming the answer is a higher damage number or a harder boss attempt.
 A practical route-planning loop
 Mark the next main objective on the Mortal Shell 2 map .
 Clear the nearest safe side path and note any Shell, weapon, upgrade, or shortcut.
 Return to the main route only after you know whether the detour changed your build.
 Keep a screenshot or written landmark for the way back to a dungeon entrance.
 This approach matters because the world is interconnected rather than a sequence of isolated levels. A route that feels like a dead end may become useful after you find a Shell or upgrade elsewhere.
 What Steam does not specify
 The store listing does not provide a complete dungeon-by-dungeon map, reward table, or recommended order. This page therefore explains the confirmed structure without inventing names or locations. Use the Mortal Shell 2 open-world size guide for the compact-world question.
