Mortal Shell 2 Weapons Guide

You always carry one melee weapon and one sidearm. There are eight of each. Pickup locations, Tarforge materials, and the player-consensus ranking below are walkthrough notes for this wiki — damage numbers on the HUD are not a published stat sheet.

How the loadout works

Combat is Resolve and posture, not a stamina bar. Weapons stay unlocked once found and can move between Shells. Tarstones are infusions, not smithing stones. Merrick in Marrow Keep sells maps that mark weapon pins if you would rather spend Coins than explore. The weapon location index lists every melee weapon and sidearm in one table.

Tarforge levels

Upgrade at the Tarforge in Marrow Keep. Cap for a first playthrough is +16. Materials climb Ventrium (+1 to +6), Laterite (+7 to +11), then Dorsalite (+12 to +16), plus Coins. Each step is about +5% damage (105% at +1, 180% at +16 on the Iconoclast table). Troubador's Lute cannot be upgraded. Smelting later refunds spent stones. Players also report a late-game item that goes past +16 — treat +16 as the advertised cap until you find that chest.

Player ranking

Not a developer list. Axatana and Black Needle sit at the top of most player write-ups. Obsidian Hammer sits at the bottom because it is slow. Patches and Shells will move this around.

Melee

  • S — Axatana. Fast dual-wield plus a heavy axe. Most lists put it here.
  • S — Black Needle. Long, safe pokes. Consistent in boss fights.
  • A — Axe and Dagger. Early fast weapon. Same niche as Axatana, weaker later.
  • A — Veteran's Battle Axe. Best heavy for stagger. Some runs find it already +4.
  • A — The Iconoclast. Safest first playthrough sword. No peak, no hole.
  • B — Clockwork Scythe. Group sweeps. Recovery is long against fast bosses.
  • B — Great Martyr's Blade. Reach and freeze. Slow startups.
  • C — Obsidian Hammer. Huge blunt hits, slowest moveset. Veteran's Axe is the friendlier heavy.

Sidearms

  • S — Triarch Repeater. Sustained fire. High ceiling with a DoT Tarstone.
  • S — Troubador's Lute. Confusion is unique. Cannot be upgraded.
  • S — Ballistazooka. Highest single-shot burst. Long recovery.
  • A — Forgotten Crossbow. Early precise pull. Cheap power spike.
  • A — Cursed Child. Sustain and execution heal.
  • A — Caged Hystrix. Tracking poison. Needs the right Tarstone.
  • B — Naylshotte. Starter shotgun. Replaced once you have a specialist.
  • B — Salvaged Trebuchaxe. Splash throw. Reload is too slow in melee.
