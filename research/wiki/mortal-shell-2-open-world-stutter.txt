 Mortal Shell 2 Open World Stutter
 Fix Mortal Shell 2 open-world stutter on PC: what each patch changed, the October 13 shader cache rebuild, Low Latency Mode, dungeon streaming and settings.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 Most performance complaints about Mortal Shell 2 on PC are traversal stutter in the open world rather than low average frame rate. Four patches since launch have targeted it directly.
 What the patches changed
 Patch Performance change 
 6 September
 Lower volumetric fog density on lower GI settings; lighting and effects optimisations
 14 September
 Crash and stability improvements
 24 September
 Low Latency Mode option (NVIDIA Reflex / AMD Anti-Lag 2); VSync tooltip with Frame Generation; hub shadow and texture optimisation
 13 October
 Rebuilt shader (PSO) caches and world HLODs; fixes for slow-streaming dungeons with “seamless dungeon streaming” on and double dungeon loading; optimisations for castle stairs, mine pillars, tomb floors and catacombs
 Settings that help
 Let shaders rebuild. After the 13 October patch, the first launch recompiles shaders. Wait on the menu before loading a save.
 Turn on Low Latency Mode if you use Frame Generation – it reduces the input lag that makes generated frames feel worse.
 Turn off seamless dungeon streaming on slower drives if dungeon doors still hang; the option trades loading screens for streaming hitches.
 Drop global illumination one step – it lowers volumetric fog density too.
 Install on an SSD. World HLOD streaming is the main source of hitching when riding Bone Portals or sprinting across Fainweald.
 Not a settings problem
 If stutter only happens entering one dungeon with Egon’s Stone equipped, that was a bug fixed on 24 September – see the Egon’s Stone hotfix .
