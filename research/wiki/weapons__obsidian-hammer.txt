 Mortal Shell 2 Obsidian Hammer
 Obsidian Hammer in Mortal Shell 2: Obsidianite Mines route, the Prophet of Profane Infinities drop, and why the Week 1 balance pass made it hit far harder.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Obsidian Hammer is the heaviest weapon you can carry, and since the Week 1 balance pass it “hits substantially harder” – the strongest buff language the developer used for any weapon.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Primary weapon – very heavy hammer
 Region
 Obsidianite Mines, Ruins of Mammon
 Unlock achievement
 Stop - Hammer Time (33.9% of players)
 Drop from
 Prophet of Profane Infinities
 Where to find it
 From the Outskirts of Mammon Beacon go west to the quarry with a large flooded pit.
 Drop into the water to enter the Obsidianite Mines .
 At the large vertical chasm, drop ledge to ledge rather than straight down.
 Defeat the Prophet of Profane Infinities at the bottom – he drops the hammer (and Paradoxical Scripture).
 How to use it
 Every hit is a stagger threat; time swings into boss recoveries rather than starting combos.
 Genessa’s Faithful Doubles copy your weapon – with the hammer they hit very hard.
 Lost Clotstone (from The Lost Child) is one of its ability Tarstones.
 Patch history
 Week 1 Update: full balance pass; “now hits substantially harder”; heavy weapon poise/damage pass.
 13 October 2026: fixed Lost Clotstone Genessa clones getting stuck with the Obsidian Hammer.
