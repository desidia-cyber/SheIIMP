 Mortal Shell 2 Gragu, the Insatiable
 Mortal Shell 2 Gragu, the Insatiable: the Heart of Vatra quest at the One-Legged Wolf Tavern, Staggering Blow, Revered Heart healing and how to play him.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 Gragu, the Insatiable is the only Shell you meet alive. He sits in the One-Legged Wolf Tavern , and getting his body means doing him a favour first. In play he trades the normal heal for a heart that refills by killing.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Region
 One-Legged Wolf Tavern, eastern Fainweald
 Requirement
 Mether’s Breath (5+ siphoned Ova) and the Heart of Vatra quest
 Unlock achievement
 Heartless (48.3% of players)
 Playstyle
 Sustain and burst stagger
 Abilities
 Staggering Blow (active): a charged punch that applies heavy stagger; the longer you charge, the more damage.
 Revered Heart (active): restores a portion of Health. Its power is replenished by slaying foes.
 How to unlock
 Siphon about five more Ova at Marrow Keep to get Mether’s Breath , which clears the corrupted growth on the eastern route.
 Speak to Gragu in the tavern (the Troubador’s Lute is on the stage in the same building).
 Head east along the cliffside path, cross the chasm and climb through the temple grounds to the top.
 Take the Heart of Vatra from the corrupted statue and survive the petrified enemies that wake up.
 Return and give Gragu the Heart. He dies, and you can claim his body.
 How to play it
 Gragu wants a steady stream of kills – he is at his best in horde areas.
 Charge Staggering Blow during a boss recovery for a near-guaranteed Break.
 The 13 October 2026 hotfix fixed a bug that blocked Gragu from being possessed and slightly adjusted his height.
