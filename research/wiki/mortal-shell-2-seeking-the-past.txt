Mortal Shell 2 Seeking the Past Achievement Not Unlocking

 Seeking the Past was a hotfix, not a hidden step
 Seeking the Past in Mortal Shell 2: max every Shell bond, talk to Genessa directly, why NG+ is needed, and the 24 September fix for the achievement not unlocking.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 Seeking the Past is one of the last achievements most players need in Mortal Shell 2, and for a month it was also one of the buggiest. Here is what it asks for and how to make sure it pops.
 What it requires
 Unlock every Shell Memory by maxing the bond with all eight Shells. Memories unlock as you raise a Shell’s bond tiers with Glimpse at Zhirelle.
 Genessa is the trap: her bond progresses by talking to Genessa directly , not to the Shellkeeper. Her five Shell Memories count toward the achievement.
 There is not enough Glimpse in one cycle for every bond. In practice you need New Game Plus , where beacon and boss Glimpse rewards respawn.
 The bug and the fixes
 Hotfix Fix 
 14 September
 Egon’s Stone fixes (the stone was involved in the broken trigger)
 24 September
 ”Fixed trophy/achievement issues with Seeking the Past”
 If you had every Memory before 24 September and the achievement did not unlock, update, then open one more Memory (or re-open the Memories menu) to retrigger the check.
 Fastest route
 Unlock all eight Shells (see all Shell locations ).
 In your first cycle, focus Glimpse on two or three Shells you actually play.
 Start NG+ and farm the respawned Glimpse from beacons and minibosses (Vrago and the Gloombound Wraith give two each).
 Talk to Genessa after each bond tier.
 Use Mether’s Severance only on Shells you will re-level – it refunds Glimpse but you lose that Shell’s tiers until you spend it again.
