 Proxima: hook first, then the Lightning hit
 Proxima's Biosampler in Mortal Shell 2: the hook bugs and the three hotfixes that fixed them, her kit, Lightning stacking and how to use the hook well.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 Proxima, the Broodseeker had the most bug reports of any Shell after launch, almost all of them about her Biosampler hook. Three hotfixes addressed it. If the hook froze you, failed to pull a boss or broke your weapon swap, this is the page.
 Proxima’s kit
 Ability Effect 
 Biosampler (active)
 Fires a hook from her left arm that pulls an enemy to you – or you to it – then strikes and inflicts Lightning
 Grafted Armour (passive)
 Base 15% chance to mitigate a melee or ranged hit, reducing it by a base 50% and preventing stagger
 Lightning
 Stacks on a target until it bursts into a damage-over-time shockwave
 The Biosampler fixes
 Hotfix Fix 
 Week 1 (29 August)
 Riposte alignment fixed on The Silent Sester
 14 September
 Proxima no longer gets stuck in the Biosampler animation
 13 October
 Cancelling the ability no longer stops weapons re-equipping; Cursed Child and Grisha summons are no longer hit by Biosampler; better interaction with The Monolith and with Bor, the Deluded Monarch
 Using Biosampler well after the fixes
 Pull ranged enemies off ledges and towers – the crossbow Sesters and Ballista Heads are ideal targets.
 Close distance on big bosses. Against The Monolith and Hexapod, hook in during recoveries instead of walking through their attacks.
 Do not hook into crowds. Pulling yourself into a pack of Unfound Path cockroaches ends badly.
 Stack Lightning fast with a quick weapon (Axe and Dagger, Iconoclast) so the shockwave triggers before the target recovers.
 Where to get her
 From the Widow’s Overlook Beacon cross the ravine to Blackridge Pass, activate its beacon, head north-east past the Treant and clear the Shattered Beacon dungeon. Full Shell page: Proxima .
