 Mortal Shell 2 Forgotten Crossbow
 Forgotten Crossbow in Mortal Shell 2: Flooded Village route and the Damp Key, crossbow use, the Shuddering Stone knockback trick, and the Old School achievement.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Forgotten Crossbow is the first sidearm most players find after the Naylshotte. It trades spread for range and precision.

 Where to find it
 Cross the rope bridges to the lower crags where the Spear Maidens patrol.
 Break the wooden boards on the stone archway to reach the tower entrance (the arch is an enchanted doorway you can walk through).
 Press the wall switch to lift the cage and ride the elevator down – it collapses on arrival.
 Clear the underground village, pulling Toxic Stalkers one at a time.
 Take the Damp Key from the box beside the back-left house.
 Unlock the middle building and take the crossbow from the table.
 Merrick gives you this map for free on your first conversation.
 How to use it
 Snipe crossbowmen and casters on ledges before you engage.
 With the Shuddering Stone Tarstone it can knock Hexapod back mid-lunge.
