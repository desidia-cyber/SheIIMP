 Mortal Shell 2 Urrig, the Executioner
 Urrig, the Executioner in Mortal Shell 2: how to reach the arena with the Immaculate Crown, the frost hammer moveset, the spin attack and the Warden's Stone.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 Urrig, the Executioner is the hammer-wielding miniboss of the Conquered Temple gate, fought between Winterglass Lake and Droeg. He is built like the Warden-type enemies you have already met but swings a hammer and inflicts Frost . His prize, the Warden’s Stone , is one of the best infusion Tarstones in the game.
 Route
 In the snowy valley past Winterglass Lake, enter Mornefrost Grave , take the right path and loot the crowned body for the Immaculate Crown . Stone hands that block paths now move aside for you.
 At the broken bridge, ring the two bells on the ground and the third bell hidden in fog on the far side (it can be hit from across the gap).
 Cross the frozen lake – enemies jump out of holes – and vault the log.
 Past the bats take the right path, then the narrow bridge on the left to its end, and drop into the arena.
 Moveset
 Attack Answer 
 Single and double hammer swings
 Parry – most of his kit is parryable
 Overhead slam
 Parry or Harden
 Multi-spin hammer whirl (unblockable)
 Dodge away; it rotates several times, so wait for the last turn
 Frost on hit
 Carry Martyr’s Ash from the valley
 Rewards
 Warden’s Stone (infusion Tarstone that adds Frost to your weapon) and roughly 6,100 Gloom. After the fight, jump on the white spring, cross the burning village and ride the giant elevator up to Ovate’s Chamber , where Ruk and the Gragu support Shell wait.
 Patch history
 The Week 1 Update retuned Urrig alongside The Warden and added new ice attacks to both.
