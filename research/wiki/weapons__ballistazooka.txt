 Mortal Shell 2 Ballistazooka
 Ballistazooka in Mortal Shell 2: Sentry's Grave route, beating the Subjugated Guardian, and why it is the hardest-hitting sidearm in the game.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Ballistazooka fires explosive projectiles and is the hardest-hitting sidearm in Mortal Shell 2. It is also the rarest sidearm achievement on Steam.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Sidearm – explosive launcher
 Region
 Sentry’s Grave, Lonesome Spire (Ruins of Mammon)
 Unlock achievement
 Speared (31.9% of players)
 Drop from
 Subjugated Guardian
 Where to find it
 From the Gate of Mammon beacon go north-west up the first staircase.
 Follow the first level clockwise to the stairs down into Sentry’s Grave .
 Break line of sight from the Ballista Heads between pushes.
 Close in on the Subjugated Guardian – up close it stops its explosive volleys and uses slow head slams you can block.
 The Ballistazooka drops on the kill.
 How to use it
 Big single-shot damage: use it to finish a staggered boss or to delete a ranged elite.
 Splash can hit you at point-blank range – fire from mid-distance.
