 Mortal Shell 2 Clockwork Scythe
 Clockwork Scythe in Mortal Shell 2: earned from Sariel, the Endless in the Chamber of Becoming, its reach, elemental fix, and the Like Clockwork achievement.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Clockwork Scythe is the reward for beating Sariel, the Endless – the same fight that unlocks the Sariel Shell. Long, sweeping arcs make it the best crowd weapon among the primaries.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Primary weapon – scythe
 Region
 Chamber of Becoming, south-eastern Mammon
 Unlock achievement
 Like Clockwork (35.8% of players)
 Drop from
 Sariel, the Endless
 Ability Tarstone
 Scholar’s Wormstone (from Sir Isaac)
 Where to find it
 Travel to The Silent Steps Beacon and head south-east to the large stone platform.
 Use the jump gate to Sariel’s area and fight until it retreats.
 Follow it up the steps into the Chamber of Becoming and push to the second encounter.
 Destroy the tombstones, defeat Sariel, the Endless and take the scythe.
 How to use it
 Wide arcs hit several enemies – excellent in the Unfound Path hordes.
 Light attack tracking improved in Balance Patch 1.
 Merrick sells a map that marks its location.
 Patch history
 24 September 2026: fixed Clockwork Scythe elemental effects not applying correctly.
