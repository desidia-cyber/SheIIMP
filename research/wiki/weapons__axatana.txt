 Mortal Shell 2 Axatana
 Axatana in Mortal Shell 2: Forgotten Tower route and Dagger Sester fight, the i-frame weapon ability with Fragile stacks, and the Dual Wielding achievement.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Axatana returns from the first game as a switchable axe/katana and has the most clearly described weapon ability in the sequel: it grants invulnerability frames and inflicts Fragile stacks.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Primary weapon – switchable axe/katana
 Region
 Forgotten Tower, near Sester’s Gate (Ruins of Mammon)
 Unlock achievement
 Dual Wielding (43.6% of players)
 Ability Tarstone
 Hexapod Core (from Hexapod)
 Where to find it
 Travel to the Sester’s Gate Beacon and enter the courtyard with the Rollerball enemy.
 Drop onto the lower ledge over the courtyard edge and turn left (not toward the abbey).
 Use the jump gate to cross the ravine into the Forgotten Tower , then a second jump gate to the far side.
 Step on the plate beside the metal door and defeat the Dagger Sester .
 Take the Axatana from the nearby niche.
 The route is easy to confuse with the path to the Revered Beacon (Cursed Child).
 How to use it
 Use the weapon ability to pass through big attacks while applying Fragile, then follow up with heavy hits.
 Light attack tracking was improved in Balance Patch 1, so the katana stance is reliable on dodgy enemies.
 Week 1 fixed its combat Tarstone active not triggering.
