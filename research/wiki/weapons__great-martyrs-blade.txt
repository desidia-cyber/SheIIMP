 Mortal Shell 2 Great Martyr's Blade
 Great Martyr's Blade in Mortal Shell 2: Martyr's Prison location via the coin pouch, the Week 1 damage buff that finally shipped, and how to use it.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Great Martyr’s Blade is the greatsword of Mortal Shell 2 and the weapon the developers had to buff twice: Balance Patch 1 announced +20% damage that never made it in, and the Week 1 Update made it “even stronger now” to compensate.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Primary weapon – greatsword
 Region
 Martyr’s Prison, Citadel of Penance / Gloomshade Grove
 Unlock achievement
 Big Boi (55.5% of players)
 Ability Tarstone
 Captive’s Scabstone (from The Nameless Captive)
 Where to find it
 Travel to the Citadel of Penance in Gloomshade Grove.
 Find the entrance to Martyr’s Prison on the Citadel’s eastern side (some routes describe a mysterious coin pouch just outside the Citadel).
 Interact with the strange coin pouch inside to be transported deeper.
 Fight through the narrow corridors and prison rooms; the blade waits in the final section.
 How to use it
 Long reach and big poise damage make it the best early stagger weapon.
 Corridors in Martyr’s Prison punish wide swings – use thrusts there.
 Pair it with Eredrim for Break-heavy runs.
 Patch history
 Balance Patch 1: announced Attack Damage +20%, which the Week 1 notes admit “didn’t actually make it”.
 Week 1 Update: deals more damage and is “even stronger now”; heavy weapon poise/damage pass.
