 Mortal Shell 2 Axe and Dagger
 Axe and Dagger in Mortal Shell 2: Chapel Key and Shrine of Trials walkthrough, the three rooms and Armored Knight, and the Deep Cuts achievement.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 Axe and Dagger is a fast paired weapon and usually the first weapon players earn rather than receive. Getting it means solving the Shrine of Trials – a short three-room dungeon near Mushroom Village.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Primary weapon – paired axe and dagger
 Region
 Shrine of Trials, Mushroom Village (Fainweald)
 Unlock achievement
 Deep Cuts (64.7% of players)
 Ability Tarstone
 Magdalena’s Memento (from Magdalena)
 Where to find it
 Find the Chapel Key on a plateau near the Mushroom Village Beacon.
 Go to the Grisha Hunter camp beside the locked tower and open the Shrine of Trials.
 Room 1: face the king pillar and walk backward – the false wall disappears.
 Room 2: push the movable stone blocks until both sit on the two pressure plates.
 Room 3: defeat the Armored Knight .
 Take the weapon from the reward area.
 How to use it
 Quick strings feed Resolve fast, which makes it a great partner for ability-heavy Shells like Tiel and Proxima.
 Equip Magdalena’s Memento for its weapon ability.
 Short reach – stay on the flank of big enemies instead of trading face-on.
