 Mortal Shell 2 The Iconoclast
 The Iconoclast in Mortal Shell 2: Harros' starting sword from the prologue, how it plays, why it counts for Lord of War, and what to replace it with.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Iconoclast is the first weapon you hold in Mortal Shell 2 – Harros’ light one-handed sword. It has no unlock achievement because you cannot miss it, but it is a genuinely good weapon for the first region and a fine parry-practice blade.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Primary weapon – light one-handed sword
 Region
 Prologue
 Unlock achievement
 None (starting gear)
 Comes with
 Naylshotte sidearm, Untarnished Seal
 Where to find it
 Received automatically during the prologue when you inhabit Harros . Nothing to hunt for.
 How to use it
 Fast light attacks are great for stun-locking the multi-legged stone enemies of the Sanguine Caverns.
 It keeps up with parry-riposte play: you are never stuck in a long recovery after a missed swing.
 It is one of the eight melee weapons for Lord of War – the others are Axe &#x26; Dagger, Veteran’s Battle Axe, Great Martyr’s Blade, Obsidian Hammer, Axatana, Black Needle and Clockwork Scythe. Sidearms do not count.
 Swap once you find Axe &#x26; Dagger (Shrine of Trials) or the Veteran’s Battle Axe (King’s Crypt) if you want more damage per hit.
