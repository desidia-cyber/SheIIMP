 Mortal Shell 2 Cursed Child
 Cursed Child in Mortal Shell 2: Revered Beacon tower route, how to survive the golden light, what its summons do, and the Beautiful Baby achievement.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Cursed Child is a summoning sidearm: instead of a projectile, it calls out creatures that fight for you. Getting it means climbing a tower while a burning golden light hunts you.

 Where to find it
 From the Sester’s Gate beacon go up the north-western stairs to the courtyard with the rolling enemy.
 Move onto the western platform and drop to the ledge with the Bone Gate.
 Head north past the hammer enemy; the dungeon door is on the right.
 The golden spotlight locks on and drains your health – break line of sight behind walls.
 Work anticlockwise up the hill to the central tower, destroy the central pillar to kill the light, and take the Cursed Child at the very top.
 How to use it
 Use the summons to split a boss’s attention while you heal or reposition.
 The 13 October 2026 hotfix fixed Cursed Child summons being targeted by Biosampler and sidearms, and stopped summons launching big bosses through collision bugs.
