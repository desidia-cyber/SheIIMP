 Mortal Shell 2 Caged Hystrix
 Caged Hystrix in Mortal Shell 2: why it only appears after beating Sariel twice, where it sits in the Chamber of Becoming, and how its spikes play.
Last verified 2026-10-15 
 The Caged Hystrix is a spike-firing sidearm hidden at the end of the Chamber of Becoming . It only appears after you have beaten Sariel, the Endless twice.
 At a glance
 Detail Value 
 Type
 Sidearm – spike launcher
 Region
 Chamber of Becoming, south-eastern Mammon

 Resource
 Resolve
 Where to find it
 Head south-east from The Silent Steps beacon to the stone platform where Sariel ambushes you.
 Drive Sariel off and follow it south; it phases through a wall and opens the Chamber of Becoming.
 Win the rematch – break all four tombstones before finishing it.
 Climb the stairs at the back of the arena; the sidearm is near the dungeon exit.
 How to use it
 Spikes are a mid-range option between the crossbow and the repeater.
 Since Balance Patch 1 it has no minimum Resolve requirement to fire.
